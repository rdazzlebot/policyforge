**A topic can now anchor NIST AI RMF identifiers**, so a generated document
genuinely cites `Govern`/`Map`/`Measure`/`Manage` rather than citing 800-53
and mentioning the AI RMF in prose. Anchoring `Govern 1` claims its seven
subcategories, the same way anchoring `AC-2` claims `AC-2(1)`.

**The crosswalk is unchanged and deliberately so.** The AI RMF is still not
something other frameworks map onto — that would assert an 800-53 control
*achieves* an AI RMF outcome, the claim NIST declined to make when it split
the Playbook out. `crosswalk seed` still refuses it, with the reason.

**Your programme percentage will fall, and the number is right.** The AI
RMF's 91 requirements are now things a topic *could* own, so they count in
the denominator; until a topic anchors them they are orphaned. On the
example registry that is 80.3% -> 73.7% with the numerator unchanged at 814.
Nothing was lost. The coverage report's scope line now names the catalogs it
counted, so a moved denominator is visible rather than inferred from a
percentage changing.
